# Business Desk staff workflow

## Request to quotation

Open **Quotation requests**, review the submitted details and private attachments, and claim follow-up responsibility. Use **Convert request to client & prepare quote** to reuse or create the matching client record. This does not issue a quotation.

Prepare a quotation by selecting a client and service catalogue lines, or add custom items. A new client can be registered inside the editor. Enter quantity, unit price and discounts in the selected currency. Add the project scope, technical specifications, exclusions, delivery, warranty/support and payment terms. Choose full payment, a deposit/balance preset or custom milestones. The schedule must equal the document total.

Save the draft, review its calculated totals, submit it for approval and let an authorised approver review the exact version. Only then issue the official document. Draft PDFs are watermarked. **Queue document email** sends through the recorded delivery queue, subject to sender configuration. Check the actual provider/delivery status.

## Client response and invoice

The linked verified client reviews the issued PDF in **Client portal**. The client must explicitly accept the terms and confirm their authority to accept that exact quotation version. Opening or downloading it does not constitute acceptance.

Convert the current approved quotation to a draft invoice and set its due date. The approved amounts are preserved, and duplicate invoice conversion is blocked. Submit, approve and issue the invoice. A manual invoice may also be prepared by an authorised administrator.

## Payment and receipt

Finance records a payment as pending using the real transaction reference, payment date, method, bank/provider and reconciliation evidence. A typed amount does not prove payment. Check the actual receipt of funds before selecting **Confirm received funds & create receipt**.

Each confirmed payment produces its own official receipt; partial payments leave the remaining balance due. Reconfirming the same payment does not create duplicate receipts. Pending or rejected payments produce no receipt. Overpayments require review.

The owner can record an actual completed full or partial refund/reversal with its reference and reason. This action records the external refund; it does not send money. The original PDF remains preserved and its verification status changes. Credit notes adjust authorised unpaid balances. Never cancel a paid invoice without reconciling the underlying payments.

## Records and reporting

Download or print the issued PDFs. The QR verification page provides authenticity and current status without exposing private client or payment details. Generate an account statement from the client's current ledger. Reports distinguish invoiced value, confirmed retained payments, reversals and outstanding receivables; currencies remain separate. Narrow filters if a CSV/PDF export reaches its 5,000-row limit.

Archive inactive clients to retain history. Use internal client notes for company-only information. Ask the owner to correct role or portal access; do not share accounts, passwords, private document URLs or sign-in codes.
