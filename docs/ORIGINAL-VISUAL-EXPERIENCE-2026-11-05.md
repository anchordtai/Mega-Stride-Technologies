Only the current scene is rendered, and only the first hero requests priority loading. No automatic carousel starts on page load.
- A seven-image service gallery uses two large lead stories, three compact stories and two wider stories. Shared component serves the homepage and solutions index. Each service detail has a distinct image, business challenge, technology focus and potential applications alongside existing scope, approach, FAQs and quotation links.
- Replaced stock image consumers throughout About, Industries, Projects, Insights, Academy, training enquiries and trade. Contact and quotation pages now have relevant image heroes. Old source assets remain preserved for history.
- Homepage project/insight feature reads actual published database content, with honest unavailable/empty states. It never fabricates a project, client, outcome or statistic. Publication data loads in parallel inside a streaming boundary.
- Academy pathways/published course cards have category-aware images. Independent-training and confirmed-enrolment notices remain. No unapproved course schedule, fee or instructor is invented.
- About purpose/ambition sections, consistent dual consultation/quotation closing actions, and long-form article typography, actual updated date, estimated reading time, sharing and published-only related articles.
- Unified responsive layouts, meaningful alt text, focusable real links/buttons, restrained image motion and reduced-motion overrides. Generated scenes are illustrative; no depicted person, facility or hardware is represented as actual company staff, premises, projects or manufacturer-branded equipment.
